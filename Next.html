📘 Lesson 04: Electron Shells

Electrons occupy energy levels around an atomic nucleus.

For the first 20 elements, the simplified shell capacities are commonly introduced as 2, 8, 8, 2.

Example: Carbon

Atomic number: 6

Electron configuration: 1s² 2s² 2p²

Shell distribution: 2, 4
