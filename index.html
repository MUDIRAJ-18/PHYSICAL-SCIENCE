Q4. Hund's Rule

Hund's rule: Electrons occupy orbitals of equal energy one at a time before pairing. The unpaired electrons have parallel spins in the ground-state arrangement.

Example: Three electrons in 2p

Q5. Draw the shape of a 1s orbital
